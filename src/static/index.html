  2. Choose the depth you need.A quick brief for orientation. In-depth research for broader evidence. A deep investigation for gaps and disagreements.
  3. Read beyond the answer.Inspect the evidence ledger and original sources. Supported means the collected passage backs a claim; it is not a guarantee that a study or conclusion is correct.
